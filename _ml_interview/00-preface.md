This book is a refresher for ML interviews, written for people who have met these topics in a course or project but need them sharp enough to explain, derive and defend under pressure. It is aimed at candidates straight out of undergrad applying to ML engineer, data scientist and applied scientist roles.

Every chapter covers the two rounds where these topics show up: the **ML fundamentals** round ("explain focal loss", "why does dropout work?") and the **ML system design** round ("design a fraud detector", where you must choose a loss, a split strategy and a rollout plan). Each topic is taught first, then tied back to how it is used in a real system.

## Anatomy of a chapter

1. **Intuition**: the idea in plain language, with an analogy where one helps.
2. **Core math**: the equations an interviewer expects you to write on a whiteboard, with every symbol explained.
3. **Code**: short, runnable Python snippets (NumPy, scikit-learn, PyTorch) that make the idea concrete.
4. **In system design**: where the topic appears when you design a production ML system.
5. **Interview Q&A**: likely questions with model answers, from warm-up to follow-up.
6. **Cheat sheet and pitfalls**: a one-page summary and the mistakes that cost candidates offers.
7. **Further reading**: the original references for that chapter.

A good way to study: read the intuition and math, close the book, then try to answer the interview questions aloud before reading the model answers.
